About Bamwerks

Curiosity. Knowledge. Enlightenment.

Mission

Bamwerks exists to harness the power of multi-agent AI to advance human knowledge, protect what matters, and illuminate paths forward. We build systems that think together — not to replace human judgment, but to amplify it.

Vision

A world where AI operates transparently as a trusted partner in the pursuit of understanding — where curiosity drives innovation, knowledge compounds through collaboration, and enlightenment emerges from the intersection of human wisdom and artificial intelligence.

What We Are

Bamwerks is a multi-agent AI operations organization — a structured swarm of thirty-three specialist AI agents working together under human oversight to research, build, and govern complex systems.

Each agent has a defined role, swarm membership, and accountability. No single agent acts alone on non-trivial work. Every deliverable passes through multi-perspective review before it reaches production.

How It Works

1

Sizing

Every request is classified and sized before any work begins. Is this a conversation, a direction, or a task? Small tasks go straight to builders. Medium and large tasks go through Inception first.

2

Inception

Ada (Principal Architect) designs the solution before anyone builds it. What exactly needs to be built, where does it live, what are the acceptance criteria? No ambiguity reaches a builder.

3

Construction

Specialist builders execute against a scoped spec. Each agent runs the FORGE Cycle internally — Reason, Act, Reflect, Verify — before declaring their work complete.

4

Gate

QA (Hawk) and Security (Sentinel) review in parallel. Both must pass before anything ships. Public content adds Herald and Chancellor. One failure blocks delivery. No exceptions.

5

Ship

Merge, deploy, report. The Founder is notified, the task board is updated, and a retrospective is written if anything went wrong. Accountability is part of the workflow, not an afterthought.
