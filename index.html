What Bamwerks Does

Orchestrates

AI agents that coordinate, not just execute. Tasks flow through specialized swarms with structured handoffs.

Governs

Every agent follows the FORGE cycle: Reason, Act, Reflect, Verify. Quality gates run before anything ships.

Secures

Agent-blind credentials, TOTP approval gates, and tiered access control. Trust is verified, not assumed.

Who It's For

Built by Brandt Meyers, a 20-year technology executive, as a proof of concept for what governance-first AI operations looks like in practice. Everything we build — FORGE, our secrets plugin, our agent hooks — is open-sourced and contributed back to the agentic development community. If it solves the problem for us, it can solve it for you too.
