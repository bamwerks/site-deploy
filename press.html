Bamwerks is a governance-first AI agent organization that operates 33 specialized agents across 8 functional swarms. Founded in February 2026, Bamwerks developed the FORGE framework (Framework for Orchestrated Reasoning, Governance & Execution) to address the 40% failure rate in AI agent deployments. The company offers governance consulting services and publishes its methodology as an open reference at bamwerks.info.
